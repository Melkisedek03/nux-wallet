Offline Mode and channel card fixes:
- Pending channel cards now show a green confirmation progress bar under the status line
- The splash now offers "Continue without internet" after 20 seconds of connecting, without waiting for the boot to fail first
- Sessions booted without internet are now marked by a chip on the Home screen, under the balance card
- Connection failures now show a clear message (e.g. Bluetooth or Location off) with what to do about it
- The Receive screen now explains why the Static Payment Code cannot be used without internet
