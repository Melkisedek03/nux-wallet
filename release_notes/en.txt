Wallet security and Channel Management fixes:
- The seed and recovery phrase are now stored in the device secure storage (Android Keystore), no longer in plain files — existing wallets are moved automatically when the app opens
- The force-close dialog now shows concrete numbers: a 144-block delay, an estimated unlock around block N, and a ~10-day note on mainnet
- The On-chain Wallet page now shows deposit progress: amount, x/6 confirmations, and the txid (tap to copy)
- Channel cards fixed: status no longer folds, a "Closing…" state shows while a close is in flight, and confirmation counts never exceed the requirement
